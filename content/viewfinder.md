Viewfinder is a personal, self-hosted tool I wrote to publish my own aviation photography
and short video. It runs unattended on a Raspberry Pi on my home network and posts on a
daily schedule.

Every photo and video it publishes is my own camera work. Viewfinder writes the text that
goes with it (caption, hashtags, alt text) and re-encodes video to each platform's format.
It does not generate imagery.

### What it does with YouTube

Viewfinder uploads my own vertical clips to my own YouTube channel as Shorts, and reads
back the resulting view, like and comment counts so I can compare how the same clip
performs on different platforms.

It authorises against one Google Account, mine. There are no user accounts, no way for
anyone else to connect a channel to it, and it is not offered as a service.
